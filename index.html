Egg Merge: Pet Match

Merge puzzle · Casual

Coming soon on Google Play

Drop cute pet bubbles, merge them and grow your way up to the King Lion!

Move the claw, drop a bubble into the box and watch it bounce and roll. When two bubbles with the same pet touch, they merge into a bigger, cuter friend: from the tiny hamster to the bunny, the fox, the panda, the tiger… all the way up to the mighty King Lion. Keep the bubbles below the red line and chase a brand-new high score.

  • 10 adorable pets to discover
  • Magic Egg, Shake Box and Clear Egg boosters
  • Missions and a 7-day Daily Gift
  • One-touch controls for all ages

Stackadel: Tower Challenge

Physics stacking · Puzzle

Coming soon on Google Play

Tap, swipe, and stack falling blocks to reach the goal!

Guide every brick into place and build a tower that stands up to rain and wind. Clear each tower on the campaign trail to earn three stars, then go endless and see how high you can climb. Timing, precision and a little bit of magic.

  • Campaign trail with 3-star towers
  • Endless mode to beat your best
  • Rain and wind that shake your tower
  • Handy boosters for tricky moments
